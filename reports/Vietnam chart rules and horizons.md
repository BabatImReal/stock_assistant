# Freeze causal chart rules before measuring Vietnam

**Recommendation:** register one price-only, close-confirmed research specification for repeated levels, triangles, rectangles, and pole-conditioned flags/pennants, and evaluate **all four outcomes, k = 3, 5, 10, 20 sessions**, without altering the production scan. These families cover the first group of named patterns Ben approved while treating overlapping drawings as one formation episode. The numerical thresholds below are **proposed design choices**, not optimized Vietnamese parameters; they require Ben's sign-off before implementation or historical results are inspected. The existing 3/5-session project remains frozen, and the 2024–2026 holdout already viewed by the project is not fresh confirmation data for these new rules. This document is a specification, **not measured Vietnamese predictive performance**: no new detector, census, four-horizon outcomes, or forward ledger was produced for it. Formal work on other markets shows why precise geometry and detection dates matter, but it does not transfer an edge to Vietnamese shares ([Lo, Mamaysky and Wang](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/19268/Lo-Mamaysky_wang_foundations.pdf); [Sullivan, Timmermann and White](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330)).

## One causal pivot clock makes the named formations testable

For each eligible stock within a continuous, reconciled adjusted-price segment, index **exchange trading sessions**, not calendar days, and use adjusted daily close `C[t]` both for anchors and for break confirmation. A high pivot at `i` is the maximum close in `i−2…i+2`, and a low pivot is the minimum; ties elect the earliest date in that five-session window. A pivot *occurs* at `i` but can first be used after close `i+2`. Successive anchors need at least three sessions of separation. While an episode is still forming, runs of same-type pivots retain the more extreme one, earliest on equality; a previously emitted signal is never revised. Bounded structures use consecutive, alternating, confirmed pivot types. This causal delay is the essential safeguard: a historically attractive second bottom cannot generate a signal on its occurrence date if two later bars were needed to know it was a bottom. The existing project's support/resistance measure already applies the same occurrence-versus-confirmation distinction; the particular two-right-bar radius here is a **proposal**, not a finding ([Lo, Mamaysky and Wang, §II](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/19268/Lo-Mamaysky_wang_foundations.pdf); [current support/resistance implementation](../src/vnstock_research/features/trend.py)).

A candidate begins no earlier than confirmation of its last required anchor. Its frozen neckline or boundary is projected using only then-known pivots; today's breaking bar never refits the line it is tested against. Record the first close **more than 0.5% beyond** a known boundary as `confirmed_up` or `confirmed_down`, with entry modeled no sooner than the next eligible open. Compare today's and yesterday's closes against the **prior known boundary snapshot**, so a shape detected after price already crossed is marked `late_or_unobservable`, not backdated. Reject and audit impossible simultaneous up/down breaks. Save each anchor's occurrence and confirmation dates, candidate date, formation ID, frozen boundary, break side and date, expiry/failure date and reason, adjustment and source versions, and detector version. `forming`, confirmed, expired, pre-confirmation invalidated, and post-confirmation failed are distinct dated states; a return inside the broken boundary by at least 0.5% within ten sessions is a later failure, not deletion of the original break. This follows the close-through-neckline convention in chart education while converting it into an auditable event rule ([StockCharts double top](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/chart-analysis/chart-patterns/double-top-reversal); [StockCharts triangle](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/chart-analysis/chart-patterns/symmetrical-triangle)).

## Four rule cards cover seven labels without seven independent bets

All percentages and durations in this table are **registration proposals**. They are deliberately a single, inspectable specification per geometry, not a search grid. No volume threshold participates in the primary price-only detector; matched-volume participation is recorded descriptively and a volume-conditioned claim would be a separately counted trial. A chart name does not set the observed breakout side ([Lo, Mamaysky and Wang](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/19268/Lo-Mamaysky_wang_foundations.pdf); [CME pattern guide](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/technical-analysis/trend-and-continuation-patterns); [StockCharts flags/pennants](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/chart-analysis/chart-patterns/flag-pennant)).

| Card | Candidate geometry and context | Confirmation and expiry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Repeated level: double/triple top or bottom** | Top: `H1–L1–H2`; bottom mirrors it. Like extrema **22–120 sessions** apart and within **1.5% of their mean**; intervening opposite swing depth **≥5%**. For a triple use `H1–L1–H2–L2–H3` or mirror: adjacent like-extrema pairs **22–90 sessions** apart, whole span **≤180**, all three within **1.5%** of their mean, both swings **≥5%**. A *reversal* context label, distinct from geometry, needs pre-formation 60-session return **≥+15% for a top or ≤−15% for a bottom**. | Double top breaks below `L1×0.995`; bottom breaks above `H1×1.005`. Triple top breaks below `min(L1,L2)×0.995`; bottom above `max(H1,H2)×1.005`. Resolve within **30 sessions** of candidate; retain a prior opposite-side level break as a contradictory resolution. If a third test arrives first, upgrade the same forming episode to a triple alias rather than minting another bet. |
| **Triangles: symmetric, ascending, descending** | **Five alternating pivots** over **15–90 sessions**. Log-price lines connect first/last high pivots (`U`) and low pivots (`L`); any middle pivot on a three-touch side is within **1%** of its line. Relative width `W=2(U−L)/(U+L)` is **≥5%** at the first anchor and **1.5% to 75% of initial width** at the last, with `U>L` throughout. Evaluate both lines at the first/last anchor dates for boundary drift: **≤1% absolute** is flat; **>+1%** rising; **<−1%** falling. Falling upper+rising lower = symmetric; flat upper+rising lower = ascending; falling upper+flat lower = descending. | Freeze lines at candidate confirmation; first close `>U(t)×1.005` or `<L(t)×0.995` decides the **observed** side. Expire at the earliest of **20 sessions** after candidate, **90 sessions** total, or the projected apex `U≤L`; no at/after-apex signal. |
| **Rectangle** | **Five alternating pivots** over **20–120 sessions**; each high within **0.75% of mean highs**, each low within **0.75% of mean lows**; `(min highs−max lows)/midpoint ≥5%`. Freeze upper `U=max(highs)` and lower `L=min(lows)` at candidate. No direction is assumed from prior trend. | First close `>U×1.005` or `<L×0.995`, within **30 sessions** after candidate and before **120 sessions** total age. Remaining inside is still `forming`, not a failed bullish forecast. |
| **Flag/pennant overlays** | Pole is the **10-session move** ending just before the pause's first anchor `s`: bull `C[s−1]/C[s−11] ≥1.10`; bear `≤1/1.10`. Pause has five alternating pivots over **12–25 sessions**, initial width **≥2%**, and every pause close retraces **≤50%** of pole height. A **flag** has both bounds moving **≥1% against** pole, final width **0.8–1.2×** initial width, pause width **≤50%** of absolute pole height. A **pennant** instead has symmetric-triangle convergence, initial width **≥5%**, final width **≥1.5%** and **≤75%** initial. | Freeze bounds at candidate. First close `>U(t)×1.005` or `<L(t)×0.995`; mark in-pole-direction break as continuation and opposite-side break as contradiction. Expire **10 sessions** after candidate, or at pennant apex. These are contextual overlays on underlying triangle/channel geometry, not separate independent events. |

The repeated-level neckline uses the more conservative of two intervening supports/resistances for a triple. A rectangle can also show repeated levels; a pennant is a pole-conditioned symmetric triangle. Preserve **all raw labels** in a stock-day fingerprint, but use one stable episode ID and a deterministic canonical label for inferential counts: flag/pennant over underlying bound geometry, triple over an unresolved double, rectangle over repeated-level when both upper and lower levels qualify. This prevents a single breakout from masquerading as several discoveries. An upside break in a conventionally bearish shape and a downside break in a conventionally bullish one remain in the denominator. These alias priorities and the candidate ID `(symbol, first-anchor date, first-anchor type)` require a hand-audited collision check before approval; if that ID is not unique, add the minimum necessary episode discriminator rather than silently merging distinct structures ([StockCharts triple bottom](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/chart-analysis/chart-patterns/triple-bottom-reversal); [current rulebook](Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md)).

## Four horizons measure tradable outcomes, not four chances to cherry-pick

For a signal at close `t`, use the current project's convention: attempt to buy at the eligible open of `t+1`, then intend to sell at the close of `t+1+k`, where **`k ∈ {3,5,10,20}` counts sessions after entry**. Thus an ordinary k=20 result exits at `t+21`, not `t+20`, and a floor-locked exit can occur later. Each `(card, variant/context/direction, k)` is a separately registered hypothesis. Record gross and **provisional** net returns, favorable/adverse excursion, actual exit offset, `known_on`, and reason for unresolved outcomes; predeclare one primary horizon or a joint decision rule before looking at results, while publishing all four cells. A bearish break in this long-only project is initially an **avoid/risk** claim, not a hypothetical short profit. Selecting the best of four horizons after inspection is another uncounted search ([existing return engine](../src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [Sullivan, Timmermann and White](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330)).

Apply the same Vietnam-specific eligibility to every horizon and to comparison rows: adjusted returns but **raw-price** ceiling entry and floor-exit checks; dated settlement sellability, historical tick/limit metadata, sale tax, broker fee, no crossing a genuine trading gap or adjustment defect, and quarantine of uncertain fills. The current engine rejects a ceiling-open buy and defers a floor-close sale up to five sessions; that is a conservative **simulation assumption**, not knowledge of queue fills. Track separately signals, entry-eligible, entered, resolved, pending, entered-but-unresolved/**STUCK**, quality-excluded, and flagged fillability, by horizon and venue/year. A position still trapped at the deferral cap is not a zero return or a deleted loser. The project's current **0.15% per-side broker fee is provisional**, while the sale tax is 0.1% of proceeds ([current return engine](../src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [project costs](../config/rules/costs.yaml); [Vietnam Ministry of Finance](https://portal.mof.gov.vn/hoidapcstc/home/cthoidap/59769); [VNDIRECT T+2 notice](https://www.vndirect.com.vn/tin_vndirect/vndirect-chinh-thuc-giao-dich-chung-khoan-t2-tu-ngay-29-08-2022/)).

**A material historical-rule conflict must be quarantined before outcome claims:** the project's `market_rules.yaml` dates UPCoM's ±10% to ±15% change to **2013-01-15**, whereas a contemporaneous State Securities Commission notice states **2015-07-01**. Price-limit-dependent UPCoM outcomes in that interval cannot be treated as verified; independently reconcile the dated rule, then amend it through a separately approved/versioned data-rule change, not by silently altering the frozen study. Other early venue/tick bands and the UPCoM reference-price calculation also need verification ([project market rules](../config/rules/market_rules.yaml); [State Securities Commission notice](https://ssc.gov.vn/webcenter/portal/ubck/pages_r/l/chitit?dDocName=APPSSCGOVVN162096245)).

For each k, compare the formation with **eligible stock-days that could have been judged under the same source-quality, liquidity, venue, regime, entry/exit, and outcome-known rules**. Show stock-specific estimates when enough independent episodes exist, else an explicitly labelled point-in-time peer/liquidity-tier fallback, then market. A stronger incremental test matches pre-signal trend/range opportunity as well; otherwise a breakout's apparent advantage could simply be ordinary momentum. Report gross/net effect size and uncertainty, hit and loss probabilities, sample and exclusion counts, and both conditional resolved-trade and entry-eligible feasible/favorable denominators. Episode overlap and concurrent market shocks require episode de-clustering and whole-date resampling. The original ten-session resampling block was chosen for 3/5 outcomes and cannot be assumed adequate for k=20 plus up to five deferred exits; register a longer-block sensitivity of **at least the maximum 26-session signal-to-exit span**, and count every variant/horizon trial in the new multiplicity family ([existing evidence gate](../src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py); [frozen protocol](../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Benjamini and Hochberg](https://rss.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x)).

## A separate research artifact preserves the frozen scan

The smallest implementation path, **subject to Ben's architectural approval**, is one research-only formation module and config outside the source trees hashed into today's stored fingerprint and return table, following the existing `structural.py` pattern. It would emit auditable event rows and derive dated fingerprint flags from those same events, with its own rule/code hash. The unchanged return engine already accepts `ks=(3,5,10,20)`; its standard persistence, however, is bound to the existing 3/5 config, so the new outcomes need a thin separately versioned research artifact/manifest. **Do not change** the frozen `patterns.yaml`, `protocol.yaml`, production scan, original E3 hypothesis log, or paper ledger simply to add chart research. Recompute the research artifact from the same promoted adjusted build; its k=3/5 values should equal the existing 3/5 engine values on matched rows. A new flag does not gain admission to the daily pick by appearing in a research table ([fingerprint implementation](../src/vnstock_research/patterns/fingerprint.py); [existing structural study](../src/vnstock_research/structural.py); [return engine](../src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [daily scan](../src/vnstock_research/report/scan.py)).

Before a census, hand-count fixtures for each card and require truncated-through-`t` runs to match full-history states at `t`; later bars must not change an earlier event. Check pivot-confirmation day versus break day, a crossing before the last anchor is knowable, ambiguous/equal pivots, opposite-side exits, expiry, aliases, gaps, exclusions and adjustment revisions. For k=10/20, exercise calendar end, ceiling no-entry, floor deferral and `known_on` gating; assert old hashes and old loaders remain unchanged. These are **proposed checks**, not tests that have passed. Use 2012–2019 for development, 2020–2023 as labelled temporal replication, and regard the viewed 2024–2026 period as spent for these new hypotheses. A freshly locked, append-only forward record after registration is the only clean confirmation path; a 20-session cohort stays pending for at least roughly 21 trading sessions, more if exit is deferred ([frozen protocol](../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [current rulebook](Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md); [existing evidence gate](../src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py)).

## Conclusion

The defensible next step is **not** to label any stock strong from these drawings yet. It is to approve or amend this one fixed price-only specification, designate a primary horizon and matched-opportunity baseline, verify the UPCoM rule and broker fee, then implement and audit the causal event census without touching production. Ben has approved researching 10/20-session outcomes alongside 3/5, but **has not yet approved these numerical geometries, alias priority, baseline strata, primary horizon, or research-module architecture**. The exact Vietnamese incidence, excess return, practical fillability, and forward reliability of every proposed pattern remain unknown until the registered study and a genuinely future record are complete.
