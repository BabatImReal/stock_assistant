# Chart patterns need proof before trades

**The current v2 report is not a reliable, actionable assessment of any Vietnamese stock's strength.** It is a useful, reproducible census of price formations and their observed break directions, but recognizable geometry, historical association, and an executable trade are three different claims. Requiring prices to stay inside the full formation repairs a concrete v1 chart defect; it does not establish that the remaining labels resemble a broker's chart calls or predict returns. The appealing historical cells come from an already-viewed, 40-trial family, with small and overlapping samples. This is an **AI broker-style critique, not a licensed broker's review, a stock recommendation, or investment advice**. ## Containment repairs one defect, not pattern meaning

The v1 audit replayed all 25 fixed cases using only information available through each event date, yet found **three of 20 confirmed formations outside their eventual walls**. V2 rejects such formations from the first to last anchor, and the three identified v1 episodes disappear. That is a meaningful geometry correction, not a measure of market-wide accuracy: no fixed, outcome-blind sample of *v2* charts has yet been visually assessed. ([V1 geometry audit](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/chart-pattern-geometry-audit-2026-10-01.md>); [v2 census](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/chart-pattern-v2-2026-10-02.md>); [v2 containment rule](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/chart_research_v2.yaml>))

The detector identifies **adjusted-close pivot geometry**, not intraday high–low shapes, volume-backed accumulation, or a complete market narrative. Its “double top/bottom” labels require repeated levels and a middle swing, but do **not** require the prior trend to qualify for admission; some reviewed v1 examples look more like trading ranges than textbook reversals. In conventional chart use, a double top follows an uptrend and completes below its intervening trough, while a double bottom follows a downtrend and completes above its intervening peak. Until v2 cases pass a blind recognition rubric, describe these as *repeated-high/low formations* and triangles as *algorithmic close-based convergences*, not certified reversal or continuation signals. ## A crossed wall is an observation, not an order

V2 records the **first close more than 0.5% beyond a frozen wall** as an observed up or down break. This causal event clock is appropriate for research. It does not establish that the break exceeds ordinary volatility or tick noise, persists, attracts matched volume, or offers a sensible entry and exit. The report correctly treats both sides as separate **long-only** hypotheses: “down” is not a tested short trade, and “up” is not automatically a buy. Later failure must be tracked after the original signal rather than used to retroactively discard it. ([V2 detector](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/chart_research_v2.py>); [v2 registration](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/chart_research_v2.yaml>); [StockCharts triangle guide](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/chart-analysis/chart-patterns/symmetrical-triangle))

The return model incorporates important Vietnam-specific constraints—next-session entry, ceiling-open rejection, T+2 sellability, sale tax, broker fees, and floor-exit deferral—but its **next-open fill remains an assumption**. Matched volume during a session does not prove Ben's intended order could fill at the opening price. A floor-held position unresolved after the five-session deferral is blanked; if those cases contain the worst losses, a conditional mean can look better than a realizable portfolio. The broker fee is provisional, and UPCoM/undated-exchange fill judgments are quarantined. HOSE and HNX/UPCoM have different ordinary price bands and exceptional trading sessions, so historical rule dating also deserves an explicit audit. ## Historical hit rates cannot certify stock strength

The predeclared family is **five formations × two observed sides × four horizons = 40 trials**, with ten sessions primary. In the later, already-viewed 2020–2023 period, descending-triangle/up shows a **+21.45 percentage-point net-positive hit edge on only 14 eligible cases**. Against the 49.98% eligible market base, that is ten positive cases; changing one case moves the observed hit rate by 7.14 points. Other cells change sign between the two historical periods, and a positive hit edge can coexist with a negative mean return. Neither period is a fresh holdout, and the already-computed 2024–2026 outcomes cannot be relabeled one. ([V2 registration](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/chart_research_v2.yaml>); [v2 census](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/chart-pattern-v2-2026-10-02.md>))

The table compares pooled eligible events with **all gated market stock-days**, not a matched same-stock, same-regime alternative or Ben's one-pick-per-day portfolio. Nearby horizons, related patterns, and signals sharing a market-shock date make nominal case counts less independent than they appear. The v2 report does not provide stock-level fallback estimates, date-block uncertainty, multiplicity-adjusted intervals, year/regime stability, or a demonstrated decision threshold. It therefore cannot answer whether a *particular stock* is strong. Its defensible claim is narrower: under the stated data and eligibility rules, these formations occurred and these conditional historical outcomes were observed. ## Blind charts, then test execution and new days

**Keep v2 research-only.** First, hash-select a fixed *v2* chart sample before opening it, stratified by formation, break side, and confirmed versus late state. Show each chart only through its event date and record, with a fixed rubric, whether the shape is recognizable, range-like or ambiguous; whether prior trend supports a reversal label; whether the boundary and 0.5% break are meaningful relative to volatility and tick size; and whether matched-volume behavior supports the interpretation. An AI reviewer can apply and disclose that rubric, but cannot turn its own visual judgment into independent broker certification. The v1 audit's event-date-only replay and deterministic selection provide a method to reuse. ([V1 geometry audit](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/chart-pattern-geometry-audit-2026-10-01.md>); [v2 census](</Users/Ben Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/chart-pattern-v2-2026-10-02.md>))

Next, on **spent history only**, publish an attrition and execution ledger by side, exchange, and year: candidate → confirmed → liquid → next-open model-fillable → resolved exit, including ceiling rejects, floor deferrals/unresolved cases, order-size and slippage sensitivities, and actual broker fees. If the blind review or execution audit exposes a real defect, freeze a separately registered v3 correction **before** viewing revised effects; do not choose thresholds because one v2 cell looked attractive. Then timestamp each new day's candidates, breaks, and “nothing strong today” decisions prospectively. Evaluate the entire fixed family after outcomes mature, with same-gate base rates, stock→tier→market fallback, date-block and multiplicity control, and one-stake-per-day execution. That sequence tests whether a chart finding survives contact with both the market and the intended decision. ## Conclusion

The crucial separation is **shape → historical association → executable prospective edge → individual-stock strength**. V2 has improved the first link and described part of the second; it has not established the latter two. The most useful immediate broker-style contribution is therefore not a stock pick but a documented blind chart judgment and an honest accounting of trades that the current return table cannot actually demonstrate.
