# Chart v2: fresh geometry and execution audit

This is a diagnostic on **already-seen 2012–2023 history**, not a new efficacy test or a stock recommendation. It changes no chart detector, threshold, return rule, or daily scan. The event sample uses no forward returns; the separate attrition audit loads the return table only to count its gate reasons, not its gross/net values.

## Fresh, outcome-blind formation sample

`scripts/audit_chart_patterns_v2.py` reuses the fixed episode-ID hash and stratification from the v1 audit, but excludes all 25 previously reviewed v1 IDs before selecting v2 events. This exclusion was added after an initial deterministic roster overlapped v1 in 22 of 25 cases; no return was opened or chart judged during that correction. The pre-2024 v2 table has only one late double-top case, already in v1, so the final roster has **24 genuinely new cases**: two confirmed up and two confirmed down for each of the five variants, plus one late case for four variants. An attempted expired-case substitution was dropped because expiry is not known at the candidate date. The final 24-row CSV is SHA-256 `eacd42192e57580feccb5435096f900a23558ef9189ad37071f3166e2642d6fb` at `data/reports/chart_audit_v2/sample.csv` (git-ignored). The selection/replay procedure was frozen in commits `ace3953`, `d1900cc`, `fb9d829`, and `c8b3ad0` before the final roster was run.

All **24/24** selected events reproduced their candidate date and first-break side/date (or late state) when v2 detection was rerun using only bars through that event date. These verify causality and make review possible; they do **not** prove that a human would recognize or trade the shapes. Per the project's no-vision-model rule, no AI image label was treated as an independent broker judgment.

The event metadata exposes a naming problem without looking at outcomes. Of the nine sampled double-level cases, only one carries the detector's own `reversal_context` tag; five are `other` and three `unknown`. Across **all 488 confirmed double-level v2 episodes dated 2012–2023**, only 77 (15.8%) carry `reversal_context` (double top 47/264; double bottom 30/224). That tag is based on the existing 60-session prior-trend rule, not a human chart verdict. Still, the current detector admits most repeated-high/low cases without the expected prior trend. “Double top/bottom reversal” therefore overstates what its geometry establishes; “repeated high/low with observed break side” is the safer research description until Ben approves a v3 label or gate.

## Ten-session model attrition

`scripts/audit_chart_execution_v2.py` joined confirmed v2 events to the **same point-in-time gate and period cutoffs** used by the descriptive k=10 table. Multiple formations on one stock-day stay distinct events. `Observable` means the pattern flag was judgeable after 125 clean sessions; `liquid` is the point-in-time universe; `resolved` means the existing model supplied a gated, known ten-session outcome. These are model stages, **not proof of an actual order fill**.

| Spent period | Confirmed breaks | Observable | PIT liquid | Model-resolved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2012–2019 | 1,005 | 674 | 428 | 369 |
| 2020–2023 | 652 | 341 | 271 | 221 |
| Total | **1,657** | **1,015** | **699** | **590** |

Among the 1,015 observable events, the gate's mutually exclusive *first recorded reasons* were: 590 resolved; 274 not liquid; 74 fillability-flagged (including UPCoM/undated exchange judgments); 41 window gaps; 26 next-open ceiling rejects; five with no next session; four not known before their period cutoff; and one whose data ended. Another 642 confirmed breaks had insufficient clean history for a known chart flag. Thirteen of the 590 resolved exits were deferred beyond the intended day. The reason order matters: for example, `not_liquid` can hide a simultaneous ceiling problem, so these counts must not be read as independent failure probabilities. The model still assumes a fill at the next open when it does not reject one; order-book depth, actual order size, slippage, and Ben's broker fee are not verified here.

## Decision before changing rules

Keep v2 research-only. The reproducible audit establishes causal event dates and a serious label/context limitation, while the attrition ledger shows that only 590 of 1,657 pre-2024 confirmed events reached a model-resolved, gated k=10 outcome. Neither result tests predictive strength. Ben's next architectural choice is whether to **rename** the broad double-level family as repeated highs/lows while preserving its population, or register a **v3 prior-trend admission rule** that would change the population. Do not select that rule by comparing the already-seen returns. For trading realism, separately obtain the intended order size and actual fee, then test opening liquidity/slippage and the dated exchange-rule assumptions before any prospective recommendation claim.
