# Vietnam chart-formation outcome protocol: 3, 5, 10, 20 sessions

## What exactly do the four horizons measure, and what must remain separate from the frozen project protocol?

### Takeaway
Treat `k = 3, 5, 10, 20` as four *registered hypotheses per formation*, not four views from which to select the best result. Extend the current entry/exit convention into a new, versioned chart-formation study; do not mutate the frozen 3/5-session hypothesis log or daily-scan contract.

### Cited Findings
- The approved existing return convention is signal at close `t`, buy at the next session's open `t+1`, intended sell at the close of `t+1+k`, with `k` counting sessions **after entry**, not after signal; current generated horizons come from `config/rules/patterns.yaml` and are 3 and 5. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [Existing patterns configuration](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/patterns.yaml)
- The frozen original protocol tests both 3 and 5 separately, counts both in its 1,554 registered hypotheses, and has distinct discover (2012–19), validate (2020–23), and now-spent holdout (2024–2026-09-10) slices. Its block and paper-trading definitions are frozen and logged. — [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Current rulebook](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md)
- The current implementation records gross/net returns, maximum favorable/adverse excursion, actual exit offset, sessions deferred, actual `known_on` date, and a reason for unresolved outcomes. The output manifest includes rule/code hashes and the fact that the broker fee is provisional. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py)
- Testing a universe of technical rules and reporting the best performer without accounting for the search can produce data-snooping bias. — [Sullivan, Timmermann and White, original paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330)

### Inferences
- Registration card for each formation should specify a single confirmation-close timestamp, first allowed entry, `k` and exact intended exit, adjusted-price basis, all-in cost, fallback/censoring states, and direction of the claim. A 20-session outcome from signal `t` is the adjusted close at `t+21` relative to the adjusted open at `t+1`, absent a deferred exit; it is **not** a close-to-close return from `t` to `t+20`. This follows the existing generator rather than redefining the two shorter outcomes. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py)
- Four horizons multiply the registered test count by four for each directional/variant/context specification. An upside and downside breakout, volume-conditioned variation, or additional detector threshold each adds further trials. Keep the chart study's registration/hash/log separate and count attempted and failed specifications, not just survivors. — [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Sullivan et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330)
- Use the same k values for descriptive per-formation gross and *provisional* net summaries, but designate one primary horizon or a predeclared joint decision rule per family before looking at outcomes. Otherwise, the most favorable of four correlated horizons becomes an uncounted selection step. — [Sullivan et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330)

### Gaps
- Ben has approved inclusion of 10/20-session research horizons, but no numeric pattern geometry, primary horizon, success threshold, or new protocol version is yet fixed; none can honestly be presented as pre-registered historical tests.

## How should settlement, price limits, gaps, and unresolved exits affect a Vietnamese outcome?

### Takeaway
Carry the current tradeable-outcome rules into all four horizons, with explicit counts for never-entered, resolved, pending, and entered-but-unresolved cases. A long horizon increases exposure to suspension, corporate-action adjustment defects, locked exits, and right-censoring; silently dropping those cases would bias the evidence.

### Cited Findings
- The project models three settlement eras by **entry** date: earliest close-sale offset 3 before 2022-08-25 and 2 from 2022-08-25. The 2022 change made shares bought on 25 August available for afternoon resale on Monday 29 August (`T+2`), whereas before the change a sale waited until `T+3`. — [Project dated rules](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/market_rules.yaml); [VNDIRECT primary broker notice](https://www.vndirect.com.vn/tin_vndirect/vndirect-chinh-thuc-giao-dich-chung-khoan-t2-tu-ngay-29-08-2022/); [HNX quarterly account of VSD rule](https://owa.hnx.vn/ftp/PORTALNEW/HEADER_IMAGES/20221014/2022%2010%2014_Derivatives%20Market%20Highlight_Q3%202022_Final.pdf)
- The current engine disallows an entry whose **raw** next-day open is at the ceiling, and postpones a sell whose **raw** intended close is at the floor for up to five sessions; returns are calculated on adjusted prices. It rejects outcomes across trading gaps, excluded rows, factor breaks, nontrading entry/exit rows, and unknown fillability. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py)
- The actual sale tax in the project is 0.1% of sale proceeds; the current 0.15%-per-side broker fee is explicitly provisional. Ministry of Finance guidance states 0.1% is charged on the transfer price for each securities transfer. — [Project costs](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/costs.yaml); [Ministry of Finance tax guidance](https://portal.mof.gov.vn/hoidapcstc/home/cthoidap/59769)
- The existing forward paper protocol distinguishes **VOID**, an attempted pick with no entry, from **STUCK**, a placed stake without a resolvable return; a stuck stake withholds the verdict instead of disappearing. The older historical gate blanks a resolved outcome when its fillability depends on an undated exchange or UPCoM. — [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Evidence gate](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py)
- **Verified conflict:** current `market_rules.yaml` says UPCoM widened from ±10% to ±15% on **2013-01-15**, but the State Securities Commission's contemporaneous notice cites HNX Decision 383/QĐ-SGDHN and says the change took effect **2015-07-01**. HNX's own retrospective agrees. Thus the current rule file's 2013–2015 UPCoM limit simulation is historically wrong; this does *not* establish the correctness of any other early-era bands. — [Project dated rules](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/market_rules.yaml); [State Securities Commission notice](https://ssc.gov.vn/webcenter/portal/ubck/pages_r/l/chitit?dDocName=APPSSCGOVVN162096245); [HNX retrospective](https://hnx.vn/vi-vn/chi-tiet-tin-60002643-0.html)
- The HOSE exchange's own current guide states an ordinary ±7% band, first-day ±20% band, and ±20% after at least 25 consecutive suspended sessions; such special bands mean a simple close/previous-close percentage test does not by itself identify a locked fill. — [HOSE price-band guide](https://staticfile.hsx.vn/Uploads/UploadDocuments/2372201/4.Bien%20do%20dao%20dong%20gia.pdf)

### Inferences
- `k=10/20` require all sessions from formation signal through actual exit to be traversable: no observation window may cross a true trading-calendar gap or adjustment defect. The same source-quality gate must be applied to signals and every matched baseline row. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [Evidence gate](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py)
- Preserve separate denominators: `signals`, `entry-eligible`, `entered`, `resolved`, `pending`, `censored after entry`, `quality-excluded`, and `fillability-flagged`; show each reason by k and venue/year. Report the probability of a **feasible and favorable** outcome among all entry-eligible signals as well as conditional net-hit probability among resolved trades. A favorable conditional number alone may hide locked losers. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [Frozen paper protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- For an entered position still at the floor after five deferrals, do not set return to zero or remove it from a forward verdict; record `STUCK` and its marked (unrealized) value separately until actual disposition or an explicitly registered censoring/bounds analysis. `pending` at the dataset right edge is different from an already known quality failure or terminal delisting. — [Frozen paper protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py)
- Keep pre-2015 UPCoM price-limit-dependent outcomes quarantined until the dated rule is separately corrected and verified against exchange histories and tests; also keep the present UPCoM unknown-reference quarantine. Do **not** edit the frozen `market_rules.yaml` in the chart-study tranche because it would change hashes and comparability of existing stored 3/5 outcomes. — [Project dated rules](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/market_rules.yaml); [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [State Securities Commission notice](https://ssc.gov.vn/webcenter/portal/ubck/pages_r/l/chitit?dDocName=APPSSCGOVVN162096245)

### Gaps
- An open-at-ceiling bar or close-at-floor bar does not reveal the order book or an investor's queue position. The engine's no-buy/defer-sell policy is a conservative simulation assumption, not a demonstrated fill probability; matched-volume and order-book evidence for partial fills is not yet available.
- The historical legal timing and exchange/tick rules for all pre-2015 HOSE/HNX/UPCoM dates have not been independently reverified here; the project rule file itself marks early bands/ticks as unverified. The current UPCoM reference calculation remains flagged by the engine.

## What is a fair base rate, and how are overlap and many tests handled?

### Takeaway
For every k, compare a formation with stock-days where the formation *could* have been judged and the same outcome was eligible, within the same point-in-time stock/group/market conditions. A later 20-session result is not twenty independent daily observations, and a family of many visually similar variants is not a family of independent tests.

### Cited Findings
- The current evidence gate joins only one adjustment build, excludes nonliquid or fillability-flagged outcomes, and enforces `known_on < before`; its fallback is stock → then liquidity tier → market when the de-clustered count is below 30. Its base rate uses all eligible judged stock-days in the same period and horizon. — [Existing evidence engine](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py); [Existing patterns configuration](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/patterns.yaml)
- The frozen protocol uses ten-session **date** blocks for two-sided resampling and Benjamini–Hochberg across all 1,554 registered hypotheses, not stock-row resampling. This was selected for 3/5 horizons and is not itself a pre-registered choice for 10/20 horizons. — [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Protocol implementation](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/protocol.py)
- Benjamini and Hochberg's original result controls the false discovery rate under stated dependence assumptions; it is not a license to omit attempted tests or ignore arbitrary correlation. Data-snooping research on technical rules evaluates the full searched rule universe. — [Benjamini and Hochberg, original article](https://rss.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x); [Sullivan et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330)

### Inferences
- Define the base pool before looking at results: same stock if enough independent formation episodes, else point-in-time liquidity tier and venue (or justified peer group), then eligible market; same date range, data quality, regime, entry/exit, k, and outcome-known cutoff. For incremental pattern value, condition both event and non-event rows on pre-signal trend/range and other prespecified formation opportunity criteria; otherwise an apparent edge may merely be momentum or regime selection. Always show the unconditional stock/group/market base as an additional context number. — [Existing evidence engine](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py); [Current rulebook](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md)
- Give each stock/formation episode a stable ID; overlapping labels from the same episode and same-direction repeat signals are one event for inferential sample size. Show raw and de-clustered N, but use episode-clustered outcomes for inference. Preserve simultaneous **different** formations as a multi-label fingerprint and disclose their dependence rather than deleting them all. — [Current rulebook](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md); [Existing evidence engine](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py)
- For k=20 plus up to five deferred exits, a ten-session resampling block cannot be assumed to cover a whole outcome exposure. Pre-register a sensitivity to longer date blocks (at least the maximum 26-session signal-to-exit span, or other defensible empirical dependence length), resampling whole cross-sections of dates together; do not report a shorter-block p-value alone. This is a proposed design choice, not a validated significance result. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- Four horizons and variant choices belong in the new study's complete multiplicity count. Because dependence may make the original BH assumptions uncertain, report effect size/uncertainty and forward replication as primary evidence; choose a documented dependence-robust or resampling multiplicity method only after registration. The inferential unit for eventual daily recommendation is **one selected signal day/stake**, separately from per-formation events. — [Benjamini and Hochberg](https://rss.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x); [Sullivan et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330); [Frozen paper protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Gaps
- The exact matched-opportunity baseline (which pre-signal trend, liquidity, volatility, and venue strata) and minimal event-episode separation are not registered. Historical outcome availability by 20-session horizon and venue must be counted before a sample-size or confidence claim.
- A 26-session block is a logical minimum span for the present exit rule, not a proof that all serial/cross-stock dependence has vanished; diagnose multiple block lengths without selecting one by favorable p-value.

## What may historical and future data legitimately establish?

### Takeaway
Use 2012–19 development and 2020–23 temporal replication only with honest labels. The 2024–2026 holdout was already inspected by the project, so fresh chart families need a newly frozen, append-only forward record after registration before any confirmation claim.

### Cited Findings
- The registered original holdout is 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-10 and its run-once rule protects only its *original* hypotheses. The project's current chart rulebook explicitly labels that period spent for new ideas, even if those ideas were not in the old 1,554-rule vocabulary. — [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Current rulebook](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md)
- The gate's `known_on < before` rule prevents a past observation's unsettled 10/20-session outcome from informing a scan or split before it had actually resolved. The original slice boundary drops outcomes not known before the next slice begins. — [Evidence gate](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py); [Protocol implementation](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/protocol.py)
- Data-snooping adjustments and genuinely new data answer different questions: the former account for searching tried rules in old samples, while the latter evaluates a fixed selected rule after it was specified. — [Sullivan et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330); [Frozen protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Inferences
- Purge each historical temporal boundary using the **actual** `known_on` (including five possible floor deferrals), not a fixed 20-session gap. A signal formed before a slice may be evaluated in the next only if formation inputs were already known at signal close and the registered split assignment permits it; do not let future-validated pivots backdate the event. — [Evidence gate](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/evidence.py); [Current rulebook](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md)
- Lock detector thresholds, aliases, priority horizon, data/adjustment versions, outcome rules, test family, and baseline before the historical chart study; timestamp every registration/amendment. Publish all four horizon cells and exclusions even if a headline focuses on one. Subsequent tuning restarts the forward clock for the changed rule. — [Sullivan et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=160330); [Current rulebook](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/reports/Vietnam%20chart%20pattern%20rulebook.md)
- At the forward lock date, a 20-session result is unavailable for roughly 21 subsequent trading sessions, more if a floor exit defers. Do not call early `pending` rows losses or suppress them from the signal census; postpone verdicts until each cohort matures and report throughput, void, stuck, and unknown rates. — [Existing return generator](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/src/vnstock_research/backtest/forward_returns.py); [Frozen paper protocol](/Users/Ben%20Nguyen/stock_assistant/stock_assistant/config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Gaps
- No new chart-family detector, 10/20 outcome table, registration hash, or forward ledger was verified or produced in this research task. Whether the existing nightly pipeline is collecting original paper rows remains an operational check, not an assumption.
