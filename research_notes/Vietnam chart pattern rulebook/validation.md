# Validation protocol for Vietnamese geometric chart patterns

## What does primary research establish about chart patterns, and what does it leave unproved?

### Takeaway
Objective chart algorithms can reveal differences in subsequent return distributions. This is evidence of information content in particular samples, not a universal probability of a rise or a demonstrated after-cost trading edge in Vietnam.

### Cited Findings
- Lo, Mamaysky and Wang defined ten geometric patterns algorithmically and compared subsequent return distributions with unconditional returns in U.S. stocks from 1962–1996. They found incremental information for some patterns, especially on Nasdaq, but explicitly said that this does not establish excess trading profit. Their tested return was one day, three days after the pattern ended; it does not validate a longer Vietnamese holding strategy. — [Original paper](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf)
- The same paper's chart definitions use consecutive local extrema; the extrema and geometry must be made explicit to escape subjective visual matching. It also found that rising versus falling volume did not usually separate the conditional distributions in its Nasdaq tests, so “volume confirmation always helps” is not an established universal rule. — [Original paper](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf)
- Sullivan, Timmermann and White evaluated a universe of 7,846 technical rules with a bootstrap that accounts for selection of the best rule. Strong historical DJIA performance did not repeat significantly in their 1987–1996 out-of-sample test or in the more tradable S&P futures sample. Their study concerns technical rules in U.S. markets, not this Vietnamese pattern catalogue. — [Original paper](https://www.kevinsheppard.com/files/teaching/mfe/advanced-econometrics/Sullivan_Timmermann_White.pdf)
- A Vietnam study tested ten *candlestick* reversal patterns on only nine HOSE stocks (2013–2018): none significantly predicted reversal at its 5% threshold. Some bullish-pattern holding-period returns were positive, but the authors omitted transaction costs. The study did not test cups, wedges, triangles, or a whole-market daily selection rule. — [Original paper](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ngo_Son3/publication/325451768_An_Analyze_on_Effectiveness_of_Candlestick_Reversal_Patterns_for_Vietnamese_Stock_Market/links/5b273c2ca6fdcc69746af008/An-Analyze-on-Effectiveness-of-Candlestick-Reversal-Patterns-for-Vietnamese-Stock-Market.pdf?origin=publication_detail)
- This project's chart-history output is explicitly descriptive: 21 patterns plus eight structural signals, k=3/5, gross, 2012–2025; the 2024–2025 cells were inspected and labelled spent. Its holdout description demonstrates the unit problem: k5 `marubozu_red+ma_50_rising+rvol_high` averaged +0.27% per de-clustered trade but −0.43% per signal day at the registered cost. — [Pattern history](../../research/reports/pattern-history.txt); [Holdout description](../../research/reports/holdout-2026-09-10-describe.txt)

### Inferences
- Test each pattern's information content and economic use separately. The pattern name and a bullish/bearish drawing are hypotheses, not labels to trust. A bearish signal is an avoidance or existing-position risk observation for a long-only user; a positive short-sale P&L is not a valid product outcome. — [Lo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf); [Project context](../../agent-memory/knowledge/context-vietnam.md)
- The existing 2012–2023 history can rank *questions to investigate*, but after extensive use of those slices and inspection of 2024 onward, no retrospective slice is a fresh confirmatory holdout for new geometric rules. The paper ledger must provide prospective confirmation. — [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Gaps
- No primary study found here estimates an after-cost, one-Vietnamese-stock-per-day probability for the geometric catalogue. The project's own forward ledger has no forward rows as recorded in [current state](../../agent-memory/CURRENT_STATE.md).
- The older studies do not validate any particular pivot tolerance, formation length, neckline rule, breakout volume threshold, or horizon for this project. These are research choices to freeze, not facts supplied by a diagram. — [Lo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf)

## How should the historical slices and forward confirmation be used?

### Takeaway
Treat all retrospective evidence through the last inspected date as exploratory for the new rules. Register immutable definitions and decisions before the first subsequent signal date, then score every eligible forward day, including failed and no-signal days, once outcomes become known.

### Cited Findings
- The existing protocol fixed discover 2012–2019, validate 2020–2023, and a holdout starting 2024 before its original tests; that holdout was run through 2026-09-10. Later pattern-history work inspected 2012–2025 by year, month and regime. — [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Pattern history](../../research/reports/pattern-history.txt)
- Its outcome gate uses `known_on`, and past cases are available to a day T only when resolved before T. The old protocol purges outcomes at slice boundaries, requires 30 de-clustered occurrences, compares with an identical-population base rate, and resamples *dates* rather than independent stock rows. — [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- The registered paper-trading unit is one proposal and one stake per signal day, with next-session entry, explicit VOID versus STUCK accounting, a minimum 30 scored signal days over at least three months, and no PASS/FAIL while the broker fee is provisional. — [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Inferences
- Use 2012–2019 to draft candidate geometry and identify data defects; use 2020–2023 as a temporal *stress description* (different years and regimes), not as independent validation of newly written rules. Treat all 2024 onward, including the already reviewed 2026 rows, as spent for new retrospective promotion. Never report a new rule as validated because it “held up” there. — [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Pattern history](../../research/reports/pattern-history.txt)
- Freeze a registration timestamp, code/rules hashes, source build, and first eligible signal session. The future test starts with **signal dates strictly after the freeze**, not merely outcomes resolving after it. A pre-freeze shape whose later breakout fires after the freeze is eligible only if the breakout and the prospective selection rule were fully fixed before that day and the formation can be reconstructed without future bars. Store its original formation and signal timestamps. — [Registered paper-trading precedent](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Lo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf)
- Keep two outputs on each date: (a) exploratory **all-stock pattern incidence** for anatomy and failure analysis; (b) a prospective, deterministic **one-pick-per-day policy** for economic assessment, if a pattern is ever proposed for action. Fix eligible universe, candidate ranking and tie breaker before scoring. A pile of profitable per-pattern events cannot substitute for the daily policy's return and no-pick frequency. — [Registered daily scan](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Holdout description](../../research/reports/holdout-2026-09-10-describe.txt)
- Use an append-only log. A later detector or fee correction starts a newly labelled version from a later forward date; prior signal dates keep the frozen version and outcome. If data are restated, retain the original snapshot, record the reconciliation and either void affected events by a pre-stated rule or report parallel corrected diagnostics; do not silently replace the forward record. — [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Registered paper-trading precedent](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Gaps
- The current notes do not confirm Ben's actual broker fee, acceptable drawdown, or long-formation holding objective. These must be fixed before any economic PASS/FAIL for new patterns. — [Project context](../../agent-memory/knowledge/context-vietnam.md); [Current state](../../agent-memory/CURRENT_STATE.md)
- The observation time needed for adequate future samples is unknown until each fixed detector's incidence is measured; 30 signal *days*, 30 de-clustered *stock events*, and a three-month calendar span are distinct counts. — [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)

## What exact design and metrics should be registered before results are run?

### Takeaway
Register a complete event-to-outcome specification, not just pattern names. Separate shape formation, actionable confirmation, failure, and actual execution; assess the one-day selection policy and downside risk as well as average return.

### Cited Findings
- The existing project requires adjusted prices, matched volume, two-source reconciliation, point-in-time stocks and liquidity, no pattern or outcome window spanning a trading-data gap, next-session entry, settlement and dated exchange limits, fees, and 0.1% sale tax. UPCoM and undated fillability have been excluded from validated outcomes. — [Project instructions](../../AGENTS.md); [Validation note](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- The old hypothesis vocabulary counted every trigger/condition/horizon combination in its multiplicity N, applied Benjamini–Hochberg FDR q=0.10 in discovery, and used 10-session date blocks. Its original horizons were only k3 and k5. — [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- The Vietnam candlestick study's omission of transaction cost makes its positive holding-period result unsuitable as a net-return benchmark. The project holdout shows costs and the chosen unit can reverse the apparent result. — [Vietnam study](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ngo_Son3/publication/325451768_An_Analyze_on_Effectiveness_of_Candlestick_Reversal_Patterns_for_Vietnamese_Stock_Market/links/5b273c2ca6fdcc69746af008/An-Analyze-on-Effectiveness-of-Candlestick-Reversal-Patterns-for-Vietnamese-Stock-Market.pdf?origin=publication_detail); [Holdout description](../../research/reports/holdout-2026-09-10-describe.txt)

### Inferences
- **Detector registration:** For every family and variant, state adjusted-OHLC pivot algorithm, confirmation lag, extrema order, geometry tolerances, minimum/maximum formation sessions, prior-trend rule, neckline/boundary construction, allowed retests and nested patterns, volume threshold on matched shares, and all unknown/gap behavior. A symmetric or inverted drawing is a separate counted variant. Log every parameter tried, including abandoned versions. — [Lo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf); [Sullivan et al.](https://www.kevinsheppard.com/files/teaching/mfe/advanced-econometrics/Sullivan_Timmermann_White.pdf)
- **Temporal states:** A candidate on day t may use only bars dated ≤t. A pivot requiring r right-hand bars becomes available on t+r, never backdated to the visual turning point. A formation is `forming` until a pre-specified close/volume condition confirms it. Store `formation_start`, `last_pivot_observed_on`, `confirmed_on`, `invalidated_on`, and first `known_on` outcome. Forming shapes and confirmed events need separate denominators; do not retrospectively call a failed candidate “never a pattern.” — [Lo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf); [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md)
- **Failures and overlap:** Specify a confirmation deadline and an invalidation boundary for forming shapes. For confirmed breakouts, predefine a false-breakout event, such as a close back inside the frozen boundary within a fixed number of *exchange sessions*; register its window and whether it is a secondary outcome, not a post hoc trigger. One stock may carry multiple family labels in one fingerprint; preserve the full overlap, choose a fixed primary event key per stock/formation, and de-cluster repeated daily observations so the same move is not counted as independent wins. State the precedence rule for conflicting bullish/bearish labels and report conflict frequency. — [Pattern-history de-clustering](../../research/reports/pattern-history.txt); [Project pattern framing](../../agent-memory/knowledge/patterns.md)
- **Horizons and no leakage:** Formation length is historical context; the forward clock begins at the first confirmed, tradable signal, not at the first trough. Preselect horizon(s) in *trading sessions* suited to long patterns (for example k5 and k20, with one named primary endpoint); every added horizon expands multiplicity. Do not infer “correct” horizon by choosing the best result later. Entry is no earlier than t+1 and an exit is no earlier than settlement permits; model limit-up nonfills, floor-locked deferrals and unresolved holdings explicitly. Purge/embargo each split by the **actual** outcome `known_on`, which may extend beyond planned k. No formation or forward window may cross an unexplained stock-session gap. A 10-session resampling block may understate dependence if k20 or deferred exits are used; preselect longer calendar-date blocks or a defensible dependence procedure before scoring. — [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- **Base rate and hierarchy:** For each exact signal-day cohort, compute the same entry/exit outcome among *all eligible stock-days where the signal was judgeable*, with the same dates, exchange eligibility, liquidity, fill gate, cost and outcome horizon. Also show a stricter matched control by prior trend, regime and liquidity to test whether the shape adds information beyond its context. Predefine stock → point-in-time liquidity tier → whole eligible market fallback and effective-sample floor; report each level's numerator, denominator and uncertainty. Do not use today's constituents or retrospectively known sectors as historical strata. — [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Project context](../../agent-memory/knowledge/context-vietnam.md)
- **Primary and secondary metrics:** Name one primary prospective endpoint before looking, preferably mean **net return per scored signal day** for the frozen one-pick policy, with the identical-population baseline. Report separately n formation candidates, n confirmed, n void nonfills, n stuck unresolved, n scored stock events and distinct signal days; gross/net hit rate and difference versus base; mean/median net return and uncertainty interval; average win/loss, payoff ratio, lower-tail quantiles, maximum adverse/favourable excursion, maximum drawdown in stakes, worst losing streak, cash tied up, no-pick share, false-breakout rate, and year/regime/stocks/exchange breakdowns. For bearish patterns, report future downside and avoidance value against the same population, without converting it to a short trade. — [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md); [Registered paper ledger](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Holdout description](../../research/reports/holdout-2026-09-10-describe.txt)
- **Controls and multiplicity:** Predeclare every family × variant × confirmation rule × context filter × horizon as the tested universe N; report all cells and null results. Preserve dependence by resampling common calendar dates, not independent stock rows, and choose the test and error threshold in advance. Add a matched placebo that draws eligible stock-days within the same month, market regime, exchange/liquidity tier and prior-trend bin, reruns the same one-pick selection count, and asks whether the geometry adds value beyond those conditions. A shuffled signal must not use future information. Choose the placebo seed and number of repetitions before seeing outcomes. — [Sullivan et al.](https://www.kevinsheppard.com/files/teaching/mfe/advanced-econometrics/Sullivan_Timmermann_White.pdf); [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)
- **Promotion rule:** Predeclare an economically meaningful positive net edge threshold, uncertainty criterion, minimum **independent signal days** and calendar span, permitted drawdown, and treatment of unresolved/void days. In historical data the result remains exploratory regardless of p-value. On untouched future days, if any criterion fails or the sample is too small, report “unproven”; do not relabel the historical best as validated. — [Sullivan et al.](https://www.kevinsheppard.com/files/teaching/mfe/advanced-econometrics/Sullivan_Timmermann_White.pdf); [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml)

### Gaps
- The research team must pick and freeze actual numeric values for geometric tolerances, pivot lag, failure window, long-pattern primary horizon, control matching bins, minimum effective sample, block length, positive net edge and drawdown threshold. The cited literature does not prescribe Vietnamese values for these choices. — [Lo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf); [Project validation](../../agent-memory/knowledge/validation.md)
- Broker fee and dated exchange rule verification remain required before an economic verdict; the existing 0.40% round trip is marked provisional. — [Registered protocol](../../config/rules/protocol.yaml); [Project context](../../agent-memory/knowledge/context-vietnam.md)
